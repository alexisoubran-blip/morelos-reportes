2. Eligibility

The Contest is open to individuals who:

  • Are legal residents of Oklahoma;
  • Are eighteen (18) years of age or older at the time of entry;
  • Have a valid and active Instagram account;
  • Maintain their Instagram account and Contest entry as public throughout the Contest Period and winner-verification period; and
  • Are able to claim their prize at a participating Supermercados Morelos location in the Oklahoma City or Tulsa area designated by Sponsor.

Employees, officers, directors, contractors, representatives, and agents of Sponsor, its affiliated companies, advertising or promotional agencies, and members of their immediate families or households are not eligible to participate. The Contest is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Contest Period

The Contest begins when the official Contest announcement is published by Supermercados Morelos and ends on September 4, 2026 at 11:59:59 p.m. Central Time (“CT”). All entries must be published and satisfy all entry requirements before the Contest deadline. Likes received after the deadline will not count toward determining the winners. Sponsor’s records and verification procedures shall determine the official closing time.

4. How to Enter

  1. Prepare an original carne asada recipe featuring products from or products sold by Supermercados Morelos.
  2. Create an original video showing the preparation of the carne asada, including the ingredients, preparation process, and final dish.
  3. Publish the video as a post or Reel on the entrant’s own Instagram account.
  4. Keep the Instagram account and Contest post public so Sponsor can view and verify the entry and its engagement.
  5. Tag @SupermercadosMorelos in the publication.
  6. Include #AquiSiSeArma in the publication.
  7. Include #AquiSiSeArmaContest or otherwise clearly disclose that the publication is a Contest entry.

An entry will not be considered valid unless all required elements can be verified by Sponsor. Unless otherwise expressly announced by Sponsor, each individual may submit one (1) Contest entry. Creating additional Instagram accounts to submit multiple entries is prohibited.

5. Entry Content Requirements

Each Contest submission must be original content created by or for the entrant with the entrant’s authorization; accurately represent the entrant’s participation; not infringe any copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, intellectual property, or other rights of a third party; not contain unlawful, threatening, defamatory, discriminatory, obscene, dangerous, or otherwise inappropriate material; not contain unauthorized commercial content belonging to third parties; comply with Instagram’s applicable Terms of Use, Community Standards, and other platform policies; comply with applicable federal, Oklahoma state, and local law; and otherwise comply with these Official Rules.

If another identifiable person appears in the submission, the entrant is responsible for obtaining any permission reasonably necessary for that person to appear in the content and for Sponsor to use the submission as described in these Official Rules. Sponsor may disqualify an entry that does not comply with these requirements.

6. How Winners Are Determined

Winners are not selected randomly.

The three (3) eligible Contest entries that receive the highest number of Valid Likes on their original Instagram Contest publication as of September 4, 2026 at 11:59:59 p.m. CT will be designated the three potential winners, subject to verification.

  • 1st Winner: eligible entry with the highest number of Valid Likes.
  • 2nd Winner: eligible entry with the second-highest number of Valid Likes.
  • 3rd Winner: eligible entry with the third-highest number of Valid Likes.

Only likes on the original qualifying Instagram post or Reel will count. Likes on reposts, Stories, duplicated posts, Facebook posts, TikTok videos, shared publications, screenshots, or content published on another account or platform will not be added to the entrant’s total. Comments, views, Reel plays, saves, shares, followers, and other engagement metrics do not count toward determining the winners.

7. Valid Likes

For purposes of this Contest, a “Valid Like” is a genuine Instagram like generated organically by an authentic Instagram user through normal use of the Instagram platform. Sponsor reserves the right to investigate and exclude likes or engagement that Sponsor reasonably determines to be fraudulent, manipulated, purchased, automated, or otherwise inconsistent with the fair administration of the Contest.

Examples of engagement that may be considered invalid include purchased likes; likes generated by bots; automated engagement; click farms; fake, fraudulent, or mass-created accounts; engagement purchased through third-party websites, applications, agencies, or services; engagement exchanges designed primarily to artificially increase Contest metrics; scripts, software, automation tools, coordinated fraudulent activity; or any other method intended to artificially manipulate the outcome.

Entrants may organically share their Contest post and invite friends, family, customers, followers, and other individuals to view and support their entry, provided that the engagement is genuine and no prohibited artificial engagement methods are used. Sponsor may investigate unusual or suspicious engagement patterns before confirming a winner. Evidence of material manipulation may result in removal of invalid likes or disqualification.

8. Official Like Count and Verification

Sponsor will document the relevant Instagram like counts at or immediately following the Contest deadline. Sponsor may use screenshots, screen recordings, public Instagram metrics, Instagram account information, platform analytics available to Sponsor or the entrant, engagement records, fraud-detection information, and/or other reasonable verification methods.

Entrants must keep their Contest post available and public through the winner-verification process and must not intentionally hide information reasonably required to verify the final like count. If requested, a potential winner must reasonably cooperate with Sponsor’s efforts to verify the authenticity of the entry and engagement. Sponsor’s verified count of Valid Likes will be the official count used to determine the Contest winners.

9. Instagram Metric Changes

Entrants acknowledge that Instagram may remove, hide, adjust, recalculate, or otherwise change engagement metrics for technical, security, fraud-prevention, policy-enforcement, or other platform-related reasons. Sponsor does not control Instagram and is not responsible for changes made by Instagram to an entrant’s like count or other engagement metrics. Likes removed by Instagram before or during Sponsor’s final verification may be excluded from the official Valid Like count. Sponsor will make reasonable efforts to document the Contest standings at the official Contest deadline.

10. Ties

If two or more eligible entries have the same number of Valid Likes and the tie affects the selection or ranking of a winner, Sponsor will award an additional shopping cart, and both tied entrants will be declared winners.

11. Prizes

There will be a minimum of three (3) prizes. Additional prizes may be awarded solely in the event of a qualifying tie as described in Section 10.

Each confirmed winner will receive one (1) Supermercados Morelos shopping cart containing approximately $120 USD worth of products for a carne asada.

Approximate Retail Value (“ARV”) per prize: $120 USD. Total Approximate Retail Value of all prizes: minimum $360 USD, subject to increase if an additional prize is awarded because of a qualifying tie.

Prize contents may include, depending on availability, meat for grilling, tortillas, salsas, beverages, charcoal, grocery products, and other carne asada-related items selected by Sponsor. Actual products may vary based on inventory and availability. No alcohol, tobacco products, lottery products, controlled substances, or other restricted merchandise will be included as part of the prize unless expressly stated by Sponsor and legally permitted.

Prizes are non-transferable. No cash redemption or substitution by the winner is permitted. Sponsor reserves the right to substitute any unavailable item with another item of equal or greater approximate retail value.

12. Winner Notification

Potential winners will be contacted exclusively by direct message on Instagram from the official @SupermercadosMorelos account. Participants are responsible for ensuring that their Instagram account can receive direct messages from Sponsor during the winner-verification period.

Potential winners must respond within forty-eight (48) hours of Sponsor’s first notification attempt. Before being confirmed, Sponsor may require reasonable evidence of identity, age, Oklahoma residency, ownership or control of the Instagram account used to enter, compliance with these Official Rules, authenticity of the entry, and authenticity of the entry’s engagement. Sponsor may also require a winner to sign an eligibility confirmation, liability release, content authorization, and/or publicity release where legally permitted.

Supermercados Morelos will never require a winner to make a payment, purchase a gift card, provide banking credentials, or pay a fee in order to claim a Contest prize. Participants should disregard messages from any account other than the official @SupermercadosMorelos Instagram account claiming to be related to winner notification.

A potential winner may be disqualified if the potential winner does not respond within forty-eight (48) hours, cannot be contacted, cannot establish eligibility, does not provide reasonably requested verification, has violated these Official Rules, or is determined to have materially manipulated Contest engagement. If a potential winner is disqualified, Sponsor may award the prize to the eligible entrant with the next-highest number of Valid Likes.

13. Prize Pickup

Confirmed winners will receive instructions from Sponsor regarding prize pickup through the official @SupermercadosMorelos Instagram account. Winners may be required to claim their prize at a participating Supermercados Morelos location in the Oklahoma City or Tulsa area selected by Sponsor. A valid government-issued photo ID may be required at pickup. Unless otherwise expressly agreed by Sponsor, transportation and any expenses associated with collecting or using the prize are the winner’s responsibility. Failure to claim a prize within the timeframe communicated by Sponsor may result in forfeiture.
