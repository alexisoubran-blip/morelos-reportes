1. Sponsor

The Contest is sponsored by Supermercados Morelos / [INSERT FULL LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [INSERT LEGAL BUSINESS ADDRESS], Oklahoma, United States, [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL] (“Sponsor” or “Supermercados Morelos”). This Contest is administered solely by Sponsor.

2. Eligibility

The Contest is open to individuals who are legal residents of Oklahoma, are eighteen (18) years of age or older at the time of entry, maintain a valid and active Instagram account, keep their account and Contest entry public throughout the Contest Period and winner-verification period, and are able to claim their prize at a participating Supermercados Morelos location in the Oklahoma City or Tulsa area designated by Sponsor.

Employees, officers, directors, contractors, representatives, and agents of Sponsor, its affiliated companies, advertising or promotional agencies, and members of their immediate families or households are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Contest Period

The Contest begins when the official Contest announcement is published by Supermercados Morelos and ends on September 4, 2026 at 11:59:59 p.m. Central Time (“CT”). All entries must be published and satisfy all entry requirements before the deadline. Likes received after the deadline will not count.

4. How to Enter

  1. Prepare an original carne asada recipe featuring products from or products sold by Supermercados Morelos.
  2. Create an original video showing the ingredients, preparation process, and final dish.
  3. Publish the video as a post or Reel on the entrant’s own Instagram account.
  4. Keep the Instagram account and Contest post public through winner verification.
  5. Tag @SupermercadosMorelos.
  6. Include #AquiSiSeArma.
  7. Include #AquiSiSeArmaContest or otherwise clearly disclose that the publication is a Contest entry.

Unless otherwise announced by Sponsor, each individual may submit one (1) Contest entry. Creating additional Instagram accounts to submit multiple entries is prohibited.

5. Entry Content Requirements

Each submission must be original content created by or for the entrant with authorization, must not infringe third-party rights, must not contain unlawful, threatening, defamatory, discriminatory, obscene, dangerous, or otherwise inappropriate material, and must comply with Instagram policies and applicable federal, Oklahoma state, and local law. Entrants are responsible for obtaining necessary permission from identifiable persons appearing in the submission.

6. How Winners Are Determined

Winners are not selected randomly.

The three (3) eligible Contest entries receiving the highest number of Valid Likes on their original Instagram Contest publication as of September 4, 2026 at 11:59:59 p.m. CT will be designated potential winners, subject to verification.

  • 1st Winner: highest number of Valid Likes.
  • 2nd Winner: second-highest number of Valid Likes.
  • 3rd Winner: third-highest number of Valid Likes.

Only likes on the original qualifying Instagram post or Reel count. Likes on reposts, Stories, duplicated posts, Facebook posts, TikTok videos, shared publications, screenshots, or content published on another account or platform will not be added. Comments, views, Reel plays, saves, shares, followers, and other engagement metrics do not count.

7. Valid Likes

A “Valid Like” is a genuine Instagram like generated organically by an authentic Instagram user through normal use of the platform. Sponsor may investigate and exclude engagement reasonably determined to be fraudulent, manipulated, purchased, automated, or inconsistent with fair administration of the Contest.

Invalid engagement may include purchased likes, bots, automated engagement, click farms, fake or mass-created accounts, third-party engagement services, engagement exchanges, scripts, software, automation tools, coordinated fraudulent activity, or other methods intended to artificially manipulate the outcome.

Entrants may organically share their Contest post and invite friends, family, customers, followers, and other individuals to support the entry, provided the engagement is genuine.

8. Official Like Count and Verification

Sponsor will document relevant Instagram like counts at or immediately following the Contest deadline using screenshots, screen recordings, public Instagram metrics, available platform analytics, engagement records, fraud-detection information, and/or other reasonable verification methods. Entrants must keep the Contest post public through winner verification and reasonably cooperate with verification requests. Sponsor’s verified count of Valid Likes will be the official count.

9. Instagram Metric Changes

Instagram may remove, hide, adjust, recalculate, or otherwise change engagement metrics for technical, security, fraud-prevention, policy-enforcement, or other platform reasons. Sponsor does not control Instagram and is not responsible for such changes. Likes removed before or during final verification may be excluded.

10. Ties

If a tie affects winner selection or ranking, tied entries will be evaluated using: Creativity and Originality (40%), Overall Presentation and Visual Execution (30%), and Execution of the Carne Asada Concept and Integration of Supermercados Morelos (30%). If a tie remains, the higher Creativity and Originality score prevails. This judging procedure applies only as a tie-breaker.

11. Prizes

There will be three (3) prizes. Each confirmed winner will receive one (1) Supermercados Morelos shopping cart containing approximately $120 USD worth of products for a carne asada. Total approximate retail value of all prizes: $360 USD.

Prize contents may include meat for grilling, tortillas, salsas, beverages, charcoal, grocery products, and other carne asada-related items selected by Sponsor, depending on availability. No alcohol, tobacco products, lottery products, controlled substances, or other restricted merchandise will be included unless expressly stated and legally permitted. Prizes are non-transferable and no cash redemption is permitted. Sponsor may substitute unavailable items with equal or greater approximate retail value.

12. Winner Notification

Potential winners will be contacted through the official @SupermercadosMorelos Instagram account and/or another contact method provided by the participant. Potential winners must respond within forty-eight (48) hours of Sponsor’s first notification attempt.

Sponsor may require proof of identity, age, Oklahoma residency, ownership or control of the Instagram account, compliance with these Official Rules, authenticity of the entry, and authenticity of engagement. Sponsor may also require eligibility, liability, content, and/or publicity releases where legally permitted.

13. Prize Pickup

Confirmed winners will receive pickup instructions and may be required to claim their prize at a participating Supermercados Morelos location in the Oklahoma City or Tulsa area. A valid government-issued photo ID may be required. Transportation and other expenses associated with claiming or using the prize are the winner’s responsibility unless Sponsor agrees otherwise.
